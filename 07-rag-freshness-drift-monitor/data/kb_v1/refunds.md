# Refunds

## Refund window
Monthly plans can be refunded within 30 days of the charge. Annual plans are refunded pro rata for unused months.

## How to request
Open Billing > Invoices, select the charge and click Request refund. Refunds are processed in 5 to 7 bussiness days.
