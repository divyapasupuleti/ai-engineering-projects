# Onboarding Guide

## Inviting teammates
Admins invite teammates from the Members page by entering email addresses. Invitations expire after 7 days and can be resent at any time.

## Roles and permissions
There are three roles: Owner, Admin, and Member. Only Owners can delete the workspace or change the billing plan. Admins can manage members and integrations.

## Connecting integrations
Integrations for Slack, Jira, and Google Drive are connected from the Integrations page. Each integration requests only the scopes it needs and can be disconnected at any time.
