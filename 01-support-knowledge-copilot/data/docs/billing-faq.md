# Billing FAQ

## Refund policy
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days. Annual plans cancelled after 30 days receive a prorated credit instead of a refund.

## Downloading invoices
Invoices are available under Settings, then Billing, then Invoice history. Each invoice can be downloaded as a PDF. Admins can also enable automatic invoice emails to a finance address.

## Changing payment method
Workspace owners can update the card on file from the Billing page. A new card is verified with a temporary authorisation of one dollar that is released within 24 hours.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails we retry the charge three times over seven days. After the final retry the workspace is moved to read-only mode until the balance is paid.
